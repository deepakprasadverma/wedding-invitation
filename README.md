## ✨ Features & Interactions

1. **"Tap to Open" Curtain & Royal Seal**:
   - Deep sage-green textured paper with embossed damask floral motifs and a central golden cord.
   - Ornate baroque medallion badge with *"TAP TO OPEN - Deepanshu & Deepanjali"*.
   - Tap parts the royal silk drapes to the left and right with floating golden confetti and soft rose petals.

2. **Bilingual Indian Welcome Card**:
   - **Hindi**: *"परमपिता परमात्मा एवं पूज्य पूर्वजों के असीम आशीर्वाद से, हम आपको दीपांशु प्रताप संग दीपांजलि कुमारी के पावन परिणय संस्कार के इस मांगलिक अवसर पर सपरिवार सादर आमंत्रित करते हैं।"*
   - **English**: *"With the divine grace of the Almighty and blessings of our beloved elders, we cordially invite you and your family to celebrate the auspicious wedding ceremony of Deepanshu Pratap and Deepanjali Kumari as they unite in holy matrimony."*
   - Auspicious invocation: *॥ ॐ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥* & *॥ अतिथि देवो भव: ॥*.

3. **Interactive Scratch to Reveal Card**:
   - Touch or mouse scratch off the sparkling golden heart to reveal the wedding date (*January 3, 2027*).
   - Generates `.ics` calendar download + Google Calendar link.

4. **Live Countdown Timer**:
   - Computes time live until January 3, 2027, 07:00 PM (*DAYS, HOURS, MINUTES, SECONDS*).

5. **Venue & Navigation**:
   - Hotel Vinayak, Hazaribagh, Jharkhand interactive map.
   - One-click *Open in Google Maps* and *Copy Address*.

6. **Interactive RSVP & Digital Pass**:
   - Guest attendance confirmation, meal preferences, and WhatsApp RSVP button.
   - Generates a personalized Digital Guest Pass with entry code `W-DD-2027` and QR code.

7. **Guestbook Wishes Wall**:
   - Real-time guest blessings wall with heart likes.

8. **Web Audio Melodies**:
   - Procedural Indian wedding flute and sitar melody with tanpura drone.

9. **Language Switcher**:
   - English, हिन्दी (Hindi), and العربية (Arabic).

## 🚀 How to Run

Open directly in your web browser:
```
C:\Users\User\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\wedding-invitation\index.html
```

Or run locally with Python:
```bash
cd "C:\Users\User\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\wedding-invitation"
python -m http.server 8080
```
Open `http://localhost:8080`.
