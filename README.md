## ✨ Features & Interactions

1. **"Tap to Open" Silk Curtain & Royal Medallion**:
   - Deep sage-green textured paper with embossed damask floral motifs and a central golden cord.
   - Ornate baroque medallion badge with *"TAP TO OPEN - Deepanshu & Deepanjali"*.
   - Tap parts the royal silk drapes with floating golden confetti and soft rose petals.

2. **Personalized Guest Links (`?guest=`)**:
   - Add query parameter like `?guest=Sharma+Family` to greet guests personally with a custom royal badge:
     - English: *"Dear Sharma Family — You and your family are cordially invited to celebrate our wedding with us 🌸"*
     - Hindi: *"आदरणीय शर्मा परिवार — आप एवं आपके परिवार का हमारे विवाह समारोह में हार्दिक स्वागत है 🌸"*
   - Pre-populates RSVP name automatically.

3. **WhatsApp Share & Custom Link Generator**:
   - Built-in generator allowing the family to type any relative's name and produce a 1-click shareable WhatsApp message with their personalized link.

4. **Interactive Scratch to Reveal Card**:
   - Touch or mouse scratch off the sparkling golden heart to reveal the wedding date (*January 3, 2027 • 07:00 PM*).
   - Generates `.ics` calendar download + Google Calendar link.

5. **Individual Function "Add to Calendar"**:
   - Separate `.ics` event files for Tilak, Haldi, Mehendi & Sangeet, and Shubh Vivah.

6. **Live Countdown Timer**:
   - Computes time live until January 3, 2027, 07:00 PM (*DAYS, HOURS, MINUTES, SECONDS*).

7. **Venue, Route Guidance & Travel Information**:
   - Hotel Vinayak, Hazaribagh, Jharkhand interactive map.
   - Distance from Hazaribagh Town Railway Station (4 km), Hazaribagh Road Station (65 km), Birsa Munda Airport Ranchi (100 km), and Bus Stand (2 km).
   - Family welcome helpline for guest assistance.
   - Accommodation booking promo code: `VINAYAK2027`.

8. **Accessible & Responsive Design**:
   - Pinch-to-zoom enabled for elder guests (`user-scalable` restriction removed).
   - Dual-language switch: English & हिन्दी (Hindi).

9. **Interactive RSVP & Unique Digital Passes**:
   - Complete checkboxes: Tilak, Haldi, Mehendi, Shubh Vivah, Grand Feast.
   - Simplified dietary options: Pure Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Jain Friendly.
   - Dynamic unique alphanumeric pass code (e.g. `W-DD-8K9F2A`) generated per guest with QR code and copy pass option.

10. **Open Graph & WhatsApp Social Preview**:
    - High-resolution 1200x630 Open Graph preview image (`og-preview.png`) ensuring beautiful card previews when links are shared on WhatsApp and social media.
